# Competitive Scrub: AI Sales Coaching & Roleplay
**Prepared for:** Peak Performers Alliance — NEPQ Sales Coach (Richards Supply Edition)
**Date:** October 28, 2026

> **About the pricing numbers:** Rilla, Siro, Craft, Second Nature and 7Q don't publish their prices. Most of the price ranges below come from third-party write-ups, and **several of those write-ups were published by competitors such as SalesAsk, Craft and AdaptClose**. Treat these numbers as rough estimates, not confirmed quotes. Amotions is the only company here that publishes its full price list.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Rilla is the market leader.** It's the best-funded company in AI coaching for in-person sales: about $70M in annual recurring revenue (Sacra estimate, April 2026), a valuation of roughly $737–757M, investors including GV and Bessemer, more than 2,000 customer accounts, and a partnership with The Home Depot. It **launched AI Roleplay in March 2026**, so it now competes directly with your roleplay feature.
- **The market has two camps:**
  1. **Recording / review tools** (Rilla, Siro, SalesAsk, Craft). These record real appointments so managers can review and score them. They're expensive (about $150–$350 per rep per month), usually require an annual contract, and often charge a $1.5K–$5K setup fee.
  2. **Roleplay / practice tools** (Hyperbound, Second Nature, Amotions). These are AI practice partners, mostly built for B2B tech sales teams rather than contractors.
- **Only one competitor is built on NEPQ: 7Q (7q.ai).** It was built in partnership with Jeremy Miner and 7th Level. It scores **Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls** against NEPQ, but it doesn't advertise a dedicated roleplay feature, and it isn't focused on contractors.
- **Where you fit:** No competitor found combines all of the following: **NEPQ method + contractor-specific personas + voice roleplay (full and coached) + live call listening + recorded-demo scoring + a CRM tie-in + free access for contractors through Richards Supply.** That combination is your opening.

## 2. Competitor Profiles

### Rilla — rilla.com (main threat)
- **What it is:** Records reps' in-person appointments and coaches them, mainly through "virtual ridealongs" (managers review a 3-minute summary of each appointment).
- **Products:** Virtual Ridealongs, Rilla Live (real-time coaching), Rilla Intelligence (company-wide analytics), and **Roleplay** (launched March 18, 2026).
  - *Replay* builds a practice roleplay from a rep's real recorded call.
  - *Training* turns uploaded playbooks into roleplay courses.
- **Industries:** Home improvement, home services (HVAC, plumbing), home building, remodeling, dental, auto, senior living, multifamily. In March 2026 it partnered with Neighborly to sell to plumbers and HVAC technicians.
- **Claims on its website:** 45% more sales, +40% close rate, +17% ticket size, new-hire training time cut in half. Setup takes "1 hr 45 min."
- **Leadership:** CEO Sebastian Jimenez, CTO Chris Martin. Advisors include the cofounder of Procore and the founders of Verkada, UiPath and Revolut.
- **Price (estimates):** About $199–$349 per rep per month (some sources say $3K–$4K per rep per year). **5-seat minimum**, paid annually up front, auto-renews, $1.5K–$5K setup fee. Average contract is about $20K per year. Roleplay has been reported as a separate add-on.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Too expensive for small contractors.
  - Depends on managers having time to review recordings.
  - Uses each company's own sales process, not a set method like NEPQ.
  - Roleplay is new and thinly documented.
  - Recording customers raises consent issues: 11 states require telling the customer.

### Siro — siro.ai (#2 in field-sales recording)
- **What it is:** A full sales-performance system for in-person, mobile and remote sellers. It's organized around three steps: **Run** (meeting prep, notes, filling in the CRM), **Evaluate** (scorecards, reports) and **Improve** (coaching, playlists, team feed, **Roleplay**).
- **Notable features:**
  - "Halftime Mode" gives coaching prompts during natural breaks in an appointment.
  - "Ask Siro" is an AI assistant.
  - Two-way sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Dynamics, LeadPerfection and Improveit360.
  - Sold inside ServiceTitan under the name "Field Pro."
  - SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA and GDPR compliant.
- **Funding:** Raised a $50M Series B in May 2025.
- **Price (estimates):** About $149–$249 per rep per month, annual contract, $1.5K–$5K setup fee.
- **Weaknesses:** Like Rilla, it depends on managers reviewing recordings. No NEPQ. Expensive.

### SalesAsk — salesask.com
- **What it is:** AI coaching built for the trades (HVAC, plumbing, **roofing**, remodeling). Its AI coach, "Coach Dean," sends reps written feedback before their next appointment.
- **Roofing focus:** Coaching on insurance and deductible conversations, handling "I'm getting three bids," and booking inspections.
- **Integrations:** ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Salesforce. Ties conversations to the revenue they bring in.
- **Price:** Says it charges per rep per month with no seat minimum, no setup fee, and monthly billing available. A directory lists it at about $50–$300 per rep per month.
- **Note:** SalesAsk publishes a lot of comparison content against Rilla and Siro, so its market claims are self-serving.

### Craft (Craft AI) — craftflow.com
- **What it is:** Coaching for both **call-center and field sales**, with real-time alerts during calls.
- **Integrations:** ServiceTitan, Leap, **AccuLynx**.
- **Price (estimates):** About $9K–$12K per year minimum (roughly $150–$200 per user per month). Setup takes about 30 days.

### 7Q — 7q.ai (only NEPQ-based competitor)
- **What it is:** AI coaching built with **Jeremy Miner / 7th Level** for NEPQ.
- **Features:**
  - **"AskJeremy"** gives coaching during and after calls.
  - **NEPQ Intelligence** scores each call by phase, from Connection through Commitment.
  - Automatic call capture, meeting summaries, and detection of who's speaking and which stage the call is in.
- **Integrations:** Zoom, Google Meet, **Microsoft Teams**, HubSpot, Zapier.
- **Target customers:** High-ticket closers selling over video and remote calls.
- **Price:** Not published.
- **Gaps:** Doesn't advertise a dedicated roleplay feature. No contractor personas. Built for remote and video calls, not field selling.

### Amotions AI — amotionsinc.com
- **What it is:** Real-time coaching that appears privately on the rep's screen during Zoom, Teams or Meet calls, plus AI roleplay and post-call insights. It also markets to roofing.
- **Published prices:**

  | Plan | Price per rep / month (billed annually) | Monthly allowance |
  |---|---|---|
  | Basic | Free | Very limited |
  | Starter | $49 | 11 hrs live coaching, 40 roleplays |
  | Professional | $99 | 23 hrs live coaching, 100 roleplays |
  | Advanced | $189 | 44 hrs live coaching, 200 roleplays |
  | Power | $349 | 81 hrs live coaching, 400 roleplays |
  | Enterprise | Custom | — |

### Hyperbound — hyperbound.ai
- **What it is:** AI roleplay for B2B sales development teams doing high-volume cold calling. It has "Screen Comprehension" (it reacts to what's shown on screen in a demo) and "Perform" (links practice results to real deal data).
- **Price:** Has a free practice tier. Paid seats are about $63–$70 per user per month at 50–150 seats (estimate).
- **Relevance to you:** Low. It isn't built for contractors.

### Second Nature — secondnature.ai
- **What it is:** Enterprise training platform with 3D avatars, gamified leaderboards and certification programs.
- **Price (estimates):** About $20K+ per year minimum, roughly $30–$40 per seat. Setup takes 1–2 months.
- **Relevance to you:** Low. It's built for large companies' training departments.

## 4. Your Advantages

1. **Price.** Free to contractors, compared with $2.4K–$4K+ per rep per year at Rilla and Siro. Competitors that require 5 seats lock out small roofing, siding and window shops entirely.
2. **NEPQ methodology.** Of the competitors reviewed, only 7Q also uses NEPQ, and it's built for remote video calls, not contractors.
3. **Practice before real calls.** Rilla and Siro mostly coach from recordings of real appointments, which means real customers. Your roleplay lets reps practice on Skeptical Sam or Price-Shopping Pete first.
4. **Part of the Richards Supply relationship.** Bundled with RichardsCRM and the supplier relationship, which builds loyalty in a way stand-alone software can't.
5. **No setup project.** It's a widget on a website. No 30-day implementation and no setup fee.

## 5. Gaps & Threats

1. **Rilla Roleplay + Home Depot.** Rilla now offers practice, recording and real-time coaching in one product, and it has a big-box retail channel. Watch for a bundled offer through suppliers.
2. **No manager view.** Every recording-based competitor sells mainly to the manager or owner, with team dashboards, playlists and reviewing every rep on every call. You have none of that today.
3. **Field recording.** Competitors record **in-person kitchen-table appointments** through a mobile app. Your Live Listening works through the browser mic, which is fine for phone calls but weaker for in-home appointments.
4. **Proof of results.** Competitors advertise numbers like "+40% close rate." You need your own early case studies.
5. **CRM reach.** Competitors connect to ServiceTitan, AccuLynx, Jobber and others. You only connect to RichardsCRM.

## 6. Recommended Next Moves (by priority)

1. **Ship Demo Analysis** (upload Teams or Zoom recordings). It's already written, but it needs the `/api/transcribe` endpoint and a redeploy. That puts you on par with Rilla, Siro and 7Q for scoring real calls.
2. **Add a simple manager/owner view.** Show each rep's daily points, scores over time and their weakest NEPQ phase. This is the main reason companies buy Rilla and Siro.
3. **Score by NEPQ phase** (Connection → Engagement → Transition → Presentation → Commitment), not just one 10-point number. This matches 7Q's main selling point.
4. **Roofing-specific personas:** an insurance-claim homeowner, a "getting 3 bids" buyer, and a storm-chaser-wary buyer. This takes on SalesAsk's roofing pitch.
5. **"Replay" mode:** turn the objections from an uploaded demo into a custom roleplay. This matches Rilla's newest feature.
6. **Gather proof:** before-and-after close rates from 3–5 Richards Supply contractors.
